FAQ — Straight answers

If you're wondering, someone else already asked.

We already have a site. Do you do rebuilds and migrations?

⌄

Yes — most of our Websites work is a rebuild or migration. We start with a crawl + parity audit, map every URL, and keep ranking through the switch with staged redirects and Search Console monitoring.

Will we be locked into WIRON hosting or a proprietary CMS?

⌄

No. You own the repo, the content, and the infra. We build on open standards (Next.js / Astro, headless CMS, standard Postgres/Redis, Terraform). You can self-host or stay with us.

Who does SEO — you or our marketing team?

⌄

Both. We ship the system (IA, templates, structured data, sitemaps, performance) and co-author the first topic clusters. Your team then publishes in the CMS — the SEO plumbing is already there.

How do Tools and Cloud fit together?

⌄

Teams that start with a portal often ask us to host it. We keep one backlog across Tools + Cloud so the product and its infra evolve together — no ticket tennis between vendors.

Do games only make sense for consumer brands?

⌄

Not at all. B2B teams use games for onboarding, training, and event engagement — interactive beats a slide deck. We measure completion and recall, not just "fun."
